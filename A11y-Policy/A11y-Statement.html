Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define the specific technical requirements for designers and engineers to systematically improve digital accessibility for individuals with disabilities. These guidelines establish three distinct levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

Kaffeinated Kodemonkey is fully conformant with the WCAG 2.2 Level AA standard (formally adopted internationally as ISO/IEC 40500:2025). Fully conformant signifies that 100% of our online content, navigation elements, interactive code modules, and user paths fully satisfy the accessibility criteria without exception.
