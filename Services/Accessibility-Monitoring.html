How It Works: Simple, Seamless Integration

  1. Tailored Footprint Mapping & Pope Tech Setup

    We analyze your digital ecosystem—including secure subdirectories, authenticated states, or Canvas LMS setups—and spin up dedicated Pope Tech monitoring profiles precisely tailored to your technical environments.

  2. Workflow & Dashboard Synchronization

    We sync automated scan data directly into your team's existing developer ecosystem (Jira, GitHub, Slack). We log directly into your Pope Tech dashboard to review alerts, transforming raw errors into native, actionable development tickets.

  3. Upskilling Internal Teams

    We provide personalized onboarding and targeted technical training sessions for your staff, ensuring your developers, designers, and content editors understand how to read scan data and prevent future errors at the source.

  4. Comprehensive Audit & Quarterly POCC Issuance

    Continuous scanning catches day-to-day regressions, but automated dashboards do not satisfy deep legal scrutiny. Every 90 days, we perform an in-depth, manual Accessibility Audit over your core environments to test interactive elements, assistive technologies, and complex code blocks. Once validated for WCAG 2.2 AA and Section 508 metrics, we issue an updated, print-ready Proof of Compliance Certificate (POCC) and digital site seal.

  5. On-Demand Expert Support

    Access our certified accessibility specialists whenever your team encounters complex ARIA patterns, dynamic JavaScript challenges, third-party integrations, or major structural redesigns.

All manual auditing, verification, and POCC issuances are certified and overseen by a DHS Certified Trusted Tester.
