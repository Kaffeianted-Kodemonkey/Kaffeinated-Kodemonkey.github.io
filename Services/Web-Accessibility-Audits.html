Choose the Audit Package That Fits Your Needs

We offer three tailored options to match your goals, timeline, and sector:

  • VPAT-Only — Ideal for RFPs, basic stakeholder requirements, or when you need a formal VPAT/ACR without certification. (No POCC seal or full report.)
  • POCC (Proof of Compliance Certificate) — Perfect for private-sector growth, SaaS platforms, and general marketing/trust needs. Includes branded certificate, accessibility statement, WCAG report, and how-to-fix guidance.
  • POCC + SAR (Section 508 Accessibility Report) — The gold standard for federal, state, higher-ed, and enterprise procurement. Adds full procurement-ready SAR with executive summary, annotated findings (screenshots + code), methodology, and extended certification.
