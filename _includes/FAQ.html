Frequently Asked Questions About Web Accessibility

A website accessibility evaluation examines a website for barriers that may prevent people with disabilities from accessing or using its content and functionality. Kaffeinated Kodemonkey combines automated scanning with manual testing using the DHS Trusted Tester process to document findings and recommend improvements.

The Proof of Compliance Certificate (POCC) is part of Kaffeinated Kodemonkey's accessibility evaluation and documentation package. The package includes an Accessibility Statement, a WCAG report, and suggested remediations. It documents the evaluation performed and its findings; it is not a government-issued certification or a guarantee of legal compliance.

Automated scanning can identify many potential accessibility issues efficiently, but it cannot reliably evaluate every accessibility requirement. Manual testing allows an evaluator to examine applicable requirements and interactions more closely. Combining both approaches provides more useful findings than relying on automated results alone.
