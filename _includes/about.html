Accessibility Expertise That Goes Beyond Automated Scans

Kaffeinated Kodemonkey is a small, specialized web accessibility agency providing accessibility evaluations, technical remediation, and consulting to help organizations build more accessible digital experiences.

Founded by a DHS Certified Trusted Tester and experienced web developer, Kaffeinated Kodemonkey combines structured accessibility testing with practical development expertise. We work to identify barriers, explain their impact, and help organizations implement meaningful improvements.

Our approach goes beyond automated scanning. We use established testing methodologies, assistive technologies, keyboard testing, and automated tools to evaluate accessibility from multiple perspectives.

From Findings to Solutions

An accessibility report is only the beginning. Our work connects evaluation findings to practical remediation, clear documentation, and ongoing accessibility improvements.

Whether your organization needs an independent evaluation, help resolving technical issues, or continued support for an accessibility program, Kaffeinated Kodemonkey provides focused expertise tailored to your project's needs.
