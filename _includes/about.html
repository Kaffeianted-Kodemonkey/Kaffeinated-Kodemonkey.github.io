Accessibility Expertise with a Practical, Hands-On Approach

Kaffeinated Kodemonkey is a small, specialized web accessibility business founded by Heather Welsh. We combine web development experience with dedicated accessibility testing to help organizations identify barriers, understand their impact, and make informed improvements.

As a DHS Certified Trusted Tester, Heather brings a structured approach to manual accessibility evaluation, supported by automated scanning and practical technical knowledge. Our work focuses on more than identifying potential issues—we help clients understand evaluation findings, prioritize remediation, and document their accessibility efforts.

We work with public agencies, educational institutions, and businesses that need experienced accessibility support, whether they are evaluating an existing website, improving development practices, or building accessibility into a new digital project.

Our approach is straightforward: practical expertise, clear reporting, and support tailored to your organization's needs.
