Automated Scanning Is a Starting Point. Manual Testing Goes Further

Automated accessibility tools can identify many potential issues across a website, helping teams establish a baseline and prioritize areas for review. However, automated results alone cannot determine whether every aspect of a website is accessible.

At Kaffeinated Kodemonkey, we combine automated accessibility scanning with manual testing using the DHS Trusted Tester process. This approach helps us investigate potential barriers, evaluate applicable accessibility requirements, and document findings that teams can use to plan remediation.

Our evaluation process includes:

  • Automated baseline scanning: Identify potential issues, recurring patterns, and pages or features that need closer attention.
  • Structured manual testing: Evaluate applicable accessibility requirements using the DHS Trusted Tester process.
  • Documented findings: Record results in a WCAG report with suggested remediation steps to help organizations understand what needs attention.

The result is a more useful picture of your website's accessibility than an automated scan alone can provide.
