Web Accessibility Services

Kaffeinated Kodemonkey helps organizations evaluate, improve, and maintain digital accessibility. Led by a DHS Certified Trusted Tester, our services include automated scanning, manual evaluation, reporting, and accessibility-first development, as appropriate to each project's scope. We support organizations working toward applicable WCAG 2.1/2.2 and Section 508 requirements, with a practical focus on identifying barriers and helping teams address them.

Accessibility Evaluation

Understand accessibility barriers through a documented evaluation. Automated scanning and manual testing methods vary by package; the evaluation scope and deliverables are agreed for each engagement.

Evaluation Options:

  • POCC & SAR Packages: Standard & Enterprise options includes a Full ADA Evaluation with Proof of Compliant Certificate (POCC) containing supporting documents and your Disability Impact Score.
  • VPAT / ACR Option: Baseline automated scanning and VPAT/ACR preparation for organizations that need accessibility conformance reporting. Manual DHS Trusted Tester evaluation is not included.

Accessibility Monitoring

Automated monitoring scans agreed website pages for detectable accessibility issues as content and code change. Scan coverage and frequency are established during scoping; automated findings may need manual verification.

Monitoring Options:

  • Ongoing Scanning: Scan configured pages for detectable issues as content and code change.
  • Multi-Site Monitoring: Support multiple websites, subject to the agreed plan and configured coverage.
  • Scheduled Audits: Manual evaluations and POCC documentation follow the selected quarterly or annual service schedule.

Accessibility-First Web Development

Accessibility should be at the core of every website design, or app development planning. We follow the POUR principles through out the entire planning and developmet process. All site get a one year POCC and hosting.

Development Options:

  • Accessible Foundations: Thoughtful page structure, navigation, forms, and reusable components.
  • Purpose-Fit Technology: Platforms and tools selected around project requirements.
  • CMS Options: Content management options considered around your team's publishing needs.
  • Accessibility in Practice: Development informed by accessibility standards, testing, and remediation experience.
