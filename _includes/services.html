Web Accessibility Services

Kaffeinated Kodemonkey helps organizations evaluate, improve, and maintain digital accessibility. Led by a DHS Certified Trusted Tester, our services combine automated scanning, structured manual testing, actionable reporting, and accessibility-first development. We support organizations working toward applicable WCAG 2.1/2.2 and Section 508 requirements, with a practical focus on identifying barriers and helping teams address them.

Accessibility Evaluation

Understand your website's accessibility barriers through a documented evaluation process. We run an auto scan establishing a baseline followed by manual testing process and log results to supply supporting documentation.

Evaluation Options:

  • POCC Package: Evaluation, Proof of Compliance Certificate, Accessibility Statement, WCAG report, and suggested remediations.
  • Supplemental Accessibility Report (SAR): Extended documentation available upon request, particularly for government entities.
  • VPAT / ACR Option: A separate evaluation and reporting option for organizations needing an Accessibility Conformance Report Only.

Accessibility-First Web Development

Build accessibility into your website from the start rather than treating it as a later repair project. We focus on semantic structure, keyboard access, understandable content, and usable interface components throughout development.

Development Options:

  • Accessible Foundations: Thoughtful page structure, navigation, forms, and reusable components.
  • Purpose-Fit Technology: Gatsby, Jekyll, and other appropriate technologies selected around project needs.
  • CMS Options: WordPress when it is the right fit for the organization and its content workflows.
  • Accessibility in Practice: Development informed by accessibility standards, testing, and remediation experience.

Includes a Proof of Compliance Certificate (POCC) first year
