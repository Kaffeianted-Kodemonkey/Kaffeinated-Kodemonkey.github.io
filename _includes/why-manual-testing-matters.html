Why Manual Testing Still Matters

AI and automated accessibility tools are useful. They can quickly surface many technical issues and support ongoing monitoring. However, even the best tools typically detect only a portion of real WCAG barriers.

The harder problems—meaningful alternative text, logical focus and reading order, complex interactive components, and actual usability with screen readers and keyboards—still require human judgment. A DHS Certified Trusted Tester follows a standardized government process and can evaluate these issues in context.

When your organization needs procurement-ready documentation, legal defensibility, or confirmation that complex interactions truly work for people with disabilities, certified manual testing remains essential. Many teams use automation for continuous checks and rely on Trusted Tester audits for high-stakes validation and formal Proof of Compliance Certificates (POCC).
