Your Trusted Partner in Web Accessibility

Founded by Heather Welsh, an independent web accessibility consultant, front-end developer, and DHS Certified Trusted Tester (ID: TT-2311-03604), Kaffeinated Kodemonkey helps organizations evaluate websites, understand accessibility findings, and plan practical improvements.

With experience in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, CMS platforms, and static site generators, Heather and her team support businesses, government agencies, educational institutions, and digital product teams. Services include scoped WCAG evaluations, developer-focused remediation guidance, ongoing monitoring, and accessibility-aware web development.

Our Commitment to Excellence

Led by a DHS Certified Trusted Tester, Kaffeinated Kodemonkey provides scoped evaluation and development services for digital teams, businesses, and public organizations. Findings and deliverables describe the agreed evaluation scope and methods.

Our Services

  • WCAG Accessibility Audit and POCC: A scoped manual evaluation using the DHS Trusted Tester Process, supported by automated scans powered by WAVE. Findings are documented in a WCAG Evaluation Audit Report (WEAR) and supporting POCC materials; deliverables depend on the selected package.
  • Accessibility Monitoring: Client teams can access the monitoring platform to review automated scan findings. Kaffeinated Kodemonkey adds a quarterly WCAG evaluation, WEAR report, and supporting POCC documentation.
  • Accessible Web Design: Website design and development with accessibility considered throughout planning, design, and implementation. Testing and documentation are defined for each project's scope.

Why Choose Us?

  • Certified Experience: DHS Trusted Tester certification and IAAP (International Association of Accessibility Professionals) membership support structured testing and clear reporting.
  • Technical Mastery: Over 9 years of deep front-end development experience means we provide developer-friendly remediation notes, not just automated scan data.
  • Clear Structure: Semantic markup, meaningful headings, and descriptive links support usability and help search engines interpret page content.
  • Proven Local Results: Trusted by regional leaders including Arapahoe County Emergency 911, the Rio Blanco Water Conservancy District, and engineering firms to deliver compliance and digital growth.
