Kaffeinated Kodemonkey provides structured web accessibility evaluations that help organizations identify accessibility barriers, prioritize remediation, and document evaluation results.

Led by a DHS Certified Trusted Tester with more than nine years of specialized accessibility experience, our evaluation process combines automated accessibility scanning with manual testing using the DHS Trusted Tester Process (DHS TTP). Findings are organized into reports and supporting documentation based on the scope and deliverables of your selected service.

Whether you need a VPAT/Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) for a procurement response or a comprehensive accessibility evaluation package, we help you understand your current accessibility status and the work needed to address identified barriers.

Why Organizations Work With Kaffeinated Kodemonkey

  • Automated and Manual Evaluation: Automated scanning establishes a baseline, while manual evaluation helps verify findings and identify accessibility barriers that automated tools cannot reliably assess.
  • Actionable Remediation Guidance: Receive documented findings and practical guidance to help development teams investigate and address identified issues.
  • Structured Documentation: Select the reports and supporting documents appropriate to your evaluation and procurement requirements.
  • Public- and Private-Sector Experience: Services are tailored to the needs of government agencies, higher education, contractors, businesses, and digital product teams.
  • Scalable Evaluation Options: Choose from focused VPAT/ACR preparation, a comprehensive POCC package, or an expanded Government and Enterprise package.

Pricing, Scope, and Service Notes

Pricing and Evaluation Scope

Published prices are starting estimates, not guaranteed final quotes. Final pricing depends on application scope, page and template coverage, interactive functionality, and agreed evaluation requirements.

Applicable criteria, testing methods, pages, templates, user journeys, and deliverables are defined for each engagement. Findings describe the documented evaluation scope and do not guarantee that every accessibility barrier has been identified.

Accessibility Documentation

A VPAT is the reporting template used to prepare an Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR). The applicable template edition and reporting criteria should match the client's requirements.

A Section 508 Supplemental Accessibility Report (SAR) is available as a separately scoped and quoted add-on. Requirements and supporting documentation will be confirmed during scoping.

Retest and Upgrade Options

The 60-day retest credit covers targeted verification of issues identified in the original evaluation, subject to engagement terms. It excludes a full re-evaluation and unrelated or later platform changes.

Eligible clients may qualify to apply 25% to 45% of their initial audit investment toward an upgraded tier within 12 months, subject to applicable terms. Confirm eligibility and credit amount during scoping.

What Your Evaluation Documents

A POCC package documents the evaluation process, the scope of testing, and the resulting accessibility findings through its included reports and supporting materials. The seal identifies the associated package; it should be read together with the evaluation documentation and applicable scope.

Accessibility is an ongoing responsibility. Websites, applications, content, and third-party components can change after an evaluation. Organizations should continue to monitor accessibility, address identified issues, and reassess material changes as appropriate.
