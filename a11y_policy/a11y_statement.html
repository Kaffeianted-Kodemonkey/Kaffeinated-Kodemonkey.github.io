Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define technical criteria for evaluating digital accessibility. WCAG has three conformance levels: A, AA, and AAA.

We are updating our WCAG Evaluation Audit Report (WEAR) and related Proof of Compliance Certificate (POCC) materials. These documents describe a scoped evaluation and its findings; they are not a government-issued certification, a guarantee of conformance, or a legal opinion. For existing evaluation details, see the POCC documentation and WCAG Evaluation Audit Report.

We work to improve the accessibility of this website and review reported barriers. Evaluation findings reflect the pages, features, methods, and date documented in the applicable report. Website changes may affect accessibility after a review.

  • We use manual evaluation methods, including the DHS Trusted Tester Process, and automated scans to identify potential barriers.
  • We use evaluation findings to plan and prioritize improvements within the reviewed scope.
  • Automated scans and scoped evaluations cannot establish that every barrier has been found or that the website satisfies every legal requirement.

Tips for Accessible Browser Customization

Skip to Main Content

Keyboard-only and screen reader users can bypass global site navigation.
Press Tab after the page loads to reveal the "Skip to Main Content" link.

Native Viewport Magnification

Use standard browser controls to zoom in or out:

  • Windows: Press Ctrl and + to zoom in, or Ctrl and - to zoom out.
  • macOS: Press Cmd and + to zoom in, or Cmd and - to zoom out.

Pointer and Focus Preferences

If you have difficulty tracking the pointer or focus outlines, your operating system's accessibility settings may let you adjust pointer size, tracking, and contrast.

  • Windows mouse settings
  • Adjust mouse tracking speed on macOS
  • Adjust pointer appearance on macOS
